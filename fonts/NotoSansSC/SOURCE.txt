The upstream variable WOFF2 unicode-range subsets were converted locally to static
400-weight TrueType subsets with FontTools 4.59.1, then merged into
NotoSansSC-Regular.ttf. Variable and layout tables that FontTools cannot merge across
unicode-range subsets were removed. The resulting font retains the glyph coverage
needed by Kaor's libass subtitle renderer.
